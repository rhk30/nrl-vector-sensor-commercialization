Low-frequency sensing, made local.

02 // Applications

Defense first. Dual-use second.

The strongest entry point is not a generic sensor company. It is a differentiated low-frequency directional sensing module for platforms where aperture, weight, deployment geometry, or autonomy matter.

Primary wedge

Defense integration

Sonobuoys, distributed ASW sensing, UUV/AUV payloads, fixed subsea surveillance, and other compact directional acoustic systems.

  • High value per sensing node
  • Clear low-frequency directionality problem
  • Natural transition through primes and OEMs
Secondary wedge

Ocean monitoring

Directional passive acoustic monitoring, offshore infrastructure, oceanographic floats, and subsea source localization.

  • Existing acoustic-monitoring procurement ecosystem
  • Bearing adds context beyond pressure-only logging
  • Potential non-defense validation environment
PHASE 01

Characterization head

Reproduce the published mesh response and integrate a modern, stable displacement readout.

PHASE 02

Moored directional node

Tank and shallow-water bearing tests against known sources; quantify sensitivity, self-noise, flow sensitivity and drift.

PHASE 03

OEM payload

Package the sensing core for a sonobuoy, UUV, seabed node, or environmental monitoring partner.

03 // IP + diligence

Strong concept. Missing field proof.

The public record is sufficient to justify technical diligence. It is not sufficient to claim a rugged underwater product. The next asset to create is credible in-water performance data.

PUBLICLY EVIDENCED

  • Fabricated silicon-nitride spider-web prototype
  • 6 mm published outer diameter and fine filament spacing
  • Approximately 2.7 m of fiber in the prototype geometry
  • Measured low-frequency directional response in air
  • Modeled underwater responsivity in the published NRL work

CRITICAL GAPS

  • In-water sensitivity, bandwidth and bearing error
  • Platform, flow and tether self-noise
  • Biofouling, corrosion and long-duration packaging
  • Optical readout SWaP, stability and cost
  • Manufacturing yield and calibration drift
  • Status of surviving NRL prototypes and unpublished data
